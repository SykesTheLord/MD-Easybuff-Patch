# Changelog

v0.1.0

Content:
 - Added "Millennium Dawn - Economy & Politics" cheat menu, opened from a decision or from the national ideas view
 - Added economy cheats for debt, treasury, corporate tax rate and corruption level
 - Added a GDP option with a warning screen explaining that calculate_gdp rebuilds GDP on the next economy refresh, and offering the persistent alternatives
 - Added interest rate testing options that drive the debt-to-GDP ratio across the 15% and 25% thresholds through MD's own premium formula
 - Added a toggle for MD_skip_treasury_cost so MD scripted costs are free
 - Added a monetary gain option granting monetary_expand_active_idea without the currency strength loss or the expansion cooldown
 - Added a money printing option that skips the political power cost and the inflation carry-over
 - Added inflation options that set inflation_rate_var and refresh the derived inflation variables, plus a central bank policy rate setter
 - Added internal faction opinion cheats, including MD's own set-all-to-maximum effect and a ruling party popularity boost
 - Added foreign influence cheats scoped to neighbours or major powers, and a domestic influence reset
 - Added options to fire and suppress MD internal politics, corruption, protest and terror events for chain testing
 - Added counter-terror capability setters and terror organisation spawn and clear options
 - Added tiered national idea columns for the economy, counter-terror capability and the power grid
 - Added free battery park, hydroelectric and nuclear reactor construction, with an immediate grid recalculation
 - Added repair decisions for the economy, politics, counter-terror and energy variable sets
